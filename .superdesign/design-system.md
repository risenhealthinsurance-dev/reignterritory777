# Reign Territory field cockpit design system

## Product context

Reign Territory is the execution layer for field representatives. REIGN OSINT supplies public-business discovery and enrichment; Territory turns that intelligence into a route, an actionable account brief, a visit disposition, follow-up/recovery work, Field AI review, and end-of-day closeout.

## Visual direction

Quiet dark command center. Keep the operational Reign identity, reduce chrome, emphasize readable hierarchy, and make one next action obvious. Use maps as context rather than the only source of truth.

## Tokens

- Background: deep navy/black.
- Surfaces: slightly lifted blue-black panels with thin low-contrast borders.
- Text: high-contrast white primary, cool gray secondary, dim gray metadata.
- Accent: cyan/blue for primary actions and route context.
- Status: green verified/success, amber cached/warning, red failure, purple enterprise/context.
- Typography: system sans for content; compact monospace for labels, timestamps, route metadata, and source badges.
- Radius: 8–16px for panels and controls; pill only for statuses.
- Touch targets: minimum 44px.
- Layout: mobile-first; 320px/390px field widths; centered max-width 390px desktop shell.

## Required states

Verified fact, confidence-labeled inference, unknown/unverified, cached/offline, needs review, loading, empty, provider warning, and success.

## Fidelity constraint

Use only this palette, typography, spacing, and component language. Do not introduce unrelated gradients, decorative marketing UI, bright light themes, or generic dashboard chrome.
